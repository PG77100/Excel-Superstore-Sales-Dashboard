# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An end-to-end interactive Excel Dashboard created to analyze Superstore sales performance, profit margins, regional growth, and customer segmentation. 

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Key KPIs

This project processes over **9,900+ order records** to help business stakeholders evaluate store profitability and identify sales bottlenecks.

* **Total Revenue / Sales**: ₹2,297,200.86
* **Total Profit**: ₹286,397.02
* **Total Orders**: 5,009
* **Total Quantity Sold**: 37,873 units
* **Average Discount**: 15.62%

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, key insights include:

1. **Category Performance**: **Technology** generated the highest sales (**₹836K**) and highest profit (**₹145K**). **Furniture** achieved high sales but significantly lower profit margins due to higher discounts/costs.
2. **Regional Analysis**: The **West Region** recorded the highest sales and profit overall, with **California** being the top-performing state.
3. **Customer Segmentation**: The **Consumer Segment** contributed the largest share of total revenue.
4. **Year-over-Year Growth**: Overall sales increased steadily year-over-year from **2015 to 2017**.

---

## 🛠️ Features & Excel Tools Used

* **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**: Dynamic aggregations for Sales and Profit by Category, Region, and Segment.
* **Interactive Slicers & Timeline**: Filters by Region, Segment, and Order Date.
* **KPI Summary Cards**: Highlighting total sales, profits, order volume, and average discounts.
* **Data Cleaning & Calculated Columns**: Standardized dates, profit margins, and month/year extraction.
